# Blueprint Model Reference

## Purpose

The Architecture Blueprint is a persistent architecture view connecting business outcomes to the information, delivery, trust, operations, technology, and decisions that support them.

## Concept tests

### Business Use Case

A use case must identify a consumer outcome, decision, accountability, or obligation. Its purpose and primary question must be understandable without naming implementation technology.

### Required Capability

A capability states what must be possible and under which functional and non-functional conditions. It is implementation-independent and reusable across use cases.

### Data Product

A product is managed information made available for a defined consumer and purpose. A database, schema, table, or dashboard is not automatically a product.

### Data Flow

A flow is a logical end-to-end movement from a real source or product to a target product or external destination. Model principal flows, not every job.

### Quality Control

A control must state the risk it addresses, how it operates, and what happens on failure or exception.

### Operational Capability

An operational capability is a repeatable ability to run, observe, recover, secure, scale, deploy, or retire the solution.

### Architecture Decision Record

An ADR captures a choice that materially shapes the architecture. It includes context, decision, rationale status, alternatives, consequences, and affected records.

### Source

A source originates or externally curates data outside the main solution. Staging, transit, orchestration, file formats, and internal stores are not sources.

### Technology

A technology is an identifiable product, service, engine, platform, or material technical pattern selected to implement or constrain the architecture.
