# Output Contract

A delivery contains:

1. the complete blueprint folder;
2. a ZIP of the folder;
3. the root `index.md`;
4. `reports/validation-report.md` and `.okf/validation-report.json`;
5. `reports/change-summary.md` for refresh operations;
6. `reports/open-questions.md` containing material unresolved items.

Raw evidence is not embedded by default. The internal evidence ledger references the source material and records hashes and locations.
