# Evidence and Confidence

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. direct customer statements about their business outcome or implementation;
2. customer-owned diagrams, configuration, and written documentation;
3. consistent statements from multiple participants;
4. interviewer summaries accepted by the customer;
5. model inference;
6. external research.

External research may define public institutions or technologies, but must not be used to invent customer-specific architecture facts.

## Confidence

- **High** — directly and clearly supported by primary evidence.
- **Medium** — supported, but boundary, terminology, ownership, or timing is incomplete.
- **Low** — plausible inference requiring early validation.

## Conflict handling

Do not overwrite a conflicting statement silently. Record both claims, their evidence, time horizon, and a validation question. A newer statement supersedes an older one only when both describe the same subject and state.
