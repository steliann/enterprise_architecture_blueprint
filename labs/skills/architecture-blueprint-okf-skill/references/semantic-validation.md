# Semantic Validation Checklist

- Does every use case explain why it exists and the decision or action it enables?
- Are similar use cases distinct by outcome, consumer, obligation, or information need?
- Are controls and operations kept out of the use-case catalogue?
- Are capabilities implementation-independent and both functional and non-functional?
- Does every product have consumers, managed information, delivery form, and ownership questions?
- Do flows begin at a real source or product and end at a product or external destination?
- Does every control define failure action?
- Does every operational capability define repeatable behaviour?
- Do ADRs distinguish confirmed decision from unknown rationale?
- Are sources outside the solution boundary?
- Are technologies identifiable products or material patterns?
- Are current, transitional, target, historical, and deprecated elements distinguishable?
- Are material unknowns visible as open questions?
