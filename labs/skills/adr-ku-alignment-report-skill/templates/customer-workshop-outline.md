### Customer-facing tone rules

- Address the customer directly and collaboratively.
- Prefer “validate together” over “challenge”.
- Prefer “in the material reviewed” over absolute statements.
- Avoid internal commercial/account-health framing.
- Do not prescribe target architecture before requirements/constraints are validated.
