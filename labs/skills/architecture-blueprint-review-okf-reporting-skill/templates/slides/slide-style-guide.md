# Slide Style Guide

This style is derived from the supplied customer-discussion deck and is intentionally light, compact and workshop-oriented.

## Canvas and typography

- 16:9 widescreen.
- Font: **Inter**.
- Main slide title: approximately 25 pt, green `#009845` or dark green `#007133`.
- Body text: dark `#333333` / `#545659`, generally 9-12 pt depending on block density.
- Small labels/metadata: 8.5-10 pt.

## Palette

- Primary green: `#009845`
- Dark green: `#007133`
- Teal: `#006580`
- Dark text: `#333333` / `#545659`
- Light green: `#EAF5EF`
- Light teal: `#E6F3F4`
- Pale blue/teal: `#EDF5F7`
- Light grey: `#F3F4F4`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Layout conventions

- Keep a clean white background.
- Use a small architecture-blueprint/review label in the upper-left or footer area.
- Prefer short action-oriented slide titles and a one-line explanatory subtitle.
- Use pale filled tiles for KPI/metadata blocks.
- Use dark green/teal bands for table headers and key callouts.
- Detailed gap slides use a left `ARCHITECTURE FINDING` column and a right `RECOMMENDATION` column, with a full-width expected-benefit/validation line at the bottom.
- Avoid dense paragraphs; convert report prose into concise slide statements.

## Detailed gap slide density

Target approximately:

- 40-65 words in the finding column;
- 55-90 words in the recommendation column;
- 3-5 action bullets;
- 1 benefit line;
- 1-2 validation questions.

If the content does not fit legibly, shorten the wording rather than reducing font size below the deck norm.

## QA

Render every slide. Check for text overflow, clipped bullets, misaligned boxes, inconsistent widths and unreadable small text. One gap per slide should remain visually balanced.
